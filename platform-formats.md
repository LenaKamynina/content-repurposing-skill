# Platform Formats

Format rules per output platform. Apply these on top of [brand-voice.md](brand-voice.md) — voice and word choice don't change between platforms, but structure, length, and pacing do.

## LinkedIn

- **Length:** 150–300 words
- **Opening:** hook on the first line — no context-setting before it. The first line must stand alone, since it's what shows before "see more" truncates the post.
- **Structure:** short paragraphs, line breaks between them — no walls of text.
- **Hashtags:** avoid in body copy; if used at all, 1–2 max, placed at the end.
- **Closing:** a direct CTA or a genuine question that invites comments.

## X / Twitter Thread

- **First tweet:** standalone hook that works without any surrounding context — the reader hasn't seen the rest of the thread yet.
- **Tweets 2–8:** one idea per tweet, direct and specific. Don't split a single idea across two tweets or cram two ideas into one.
- **Length:** each tweet under 280 characters.
- **No filler openers:** never start with "Let me explain," "Here's a thread:," or similar throat-clearing.
- **Final tweet:** a call to action or a memorable summary statement.
- **Feel:** the thread should read like a conversation, not a slide deck — avoid overly formal, enumerated "Point 1 / Point 2" phrasing.

## Newsletter Section

- **Length:** 300–500 words
- **Point of view:** second person ("you") or first person — pick one per section, don't mix.
- **Structure:** context → insight → implication or action.
- **Focus:** one key takeaway per section — resist the urge to pack in multiple points.
- **Links:** max 1–2 per section — don't over-link.
- **Subheadings:** optional, useful for longer sections to break up the read.
